Scientific background

HUNTER is a Pre-Phase A twin-spacecraft heliophysics mission concept investigating how solar-wind dynamic-pressure events affect Earth’s high-latitude ionosphere and thermosphere. Coordinated measurements of plasma, neutral populations and electromagnetic fields would characterize density variability, field-aligned currents and plasma inflow and outflow in the polar regions.

Technical contributions

Led science implementation and instrumentation within a five-member team, developing measurement requirements through the Science Traceability Matrix and evaluating magnetometers, electric-field instruments, Langmuir probes and ion–neutral mass spectrometers. Assessed sensor placement, magnetic cleanliness, calibration considerations and science data budgets. Responsibilities also covered S-band ground communications and C&DH architecture, including SpaceWire payload interfaces, GNSS time synchronization and onboard storage, alongside green-monopropellant trade studies for orbital phasing and formation maintenance.
