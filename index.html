What you’re seeing

A star shaped by magnetism.

Extreme-ultraviolet views reveal the Sun’s hot outer atmosphere: bright plasma loops trace magnetic structures above the surface. The colors represent different wavelengths and temperatures, revealing details our eyes cannot see.

About the SDO imagery ↗
From the Sun to Earth

Solar activity reaches home.

Solar flares release radiation that can disturb Earth’s ionosphere and radio communications. Associated coronal mass ejections can send magnetized plasma toward Earth; when it couples with our magnetic field, it can drive geomagnetic storms, auroras and disruptions to satellites, navigation and power systems.
