Scientific background

The triangular Lagrange points form equilateral configurations with two orbiting bodies, but their stability depends on the system’s mass ratio. TALOS compares the linearly stable Sun–Earth reference with the unstable Pluto–Charon case to understand how small errors evolve and how much correction is needed to remain inside a prescribed region.

Technical contributions

Developed a MATLAB study using the planar circular restricted three-body problem, linearized dynamics and state transition matrices. Formulated a fixed-maneuver-time, minimum-total-impulse optimization with sampled position constraints, checked the resulting controls with nonlinear propagation, and tested sensitivity to the allowed radius, perturbation direction and first available maneuver time.

Project report

Minimum-total-impulse station-keeping near L4 / L5

The selected Sun–Earth cases require no correction over the finite horizon. Pluto–Charon reference solutions use a single initial impulse and pass the sampled nonlinear radius test. The report details the model assumptions, optimization, validation and sensitivity studies.
