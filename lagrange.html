Scientific background

This project investigates how small initial-state perturbations and time-dependent gravitational effects influence particle motion near the Earth–Moon L4 and L5 points. Comparing autonomous and time-varying models helps assess trajectory sensitivity and predictability near these equilibrium regions.

Technical contributions

Developed MATLAB simulations of nominal and perturbed trajectories using the Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem and a periodically forced extension. Propagated state transition matrices to quantify perturbation growth, compared trajectory separation and STM norms, and verified numerical accuracy through Jacobi-integral conservation in the autonomous model.

Trajectory simulations

Earth–Moon L4/L5 trajectory evolution

Compares trajectories near Earth–Moon L4 and L5 in autonomous and periodically forced models. Shows how small perturbations affect the evolving paths.

Three-dimensional L4/L5 trajectories

Propagates nominal and perturbed trajectories near L4/L5 in three dimensions. Highlights out-of-plane motion and nearby-trajectory separation in the rotating frame.
