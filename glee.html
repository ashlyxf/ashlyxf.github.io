Scientific background

GLEE is a NASA-funded, student-led lunar mission aiming to deploy more than 100 LunaSats across the lunar surface. Distributed measurements of magnetic fields, surface vibrations, temperature and regolith properties would help characterize the Moon’s surface environment and its spatial variability.

Technical contributions

Co-led HDM development within a 17-member team, designing electrical schematics and PCB layouts. Developed and bench-tested C/C++ firmware for the central lead-screw/forklift elevator, barrel indexing and HDM rotation using Arduino Nano control with TMC5160T Pro V1.0 and TMC2209 V985 stepper drivers. Electrical integration covered the Cytron MDD10A motor controller and dual-servo arming, sweep and reset sequences. Supported actuator-timing, electrical-interface and mechanical-alignment testing, verification planning and undergraduate mentoring.
